THE MEANING OF ALIFEROUS

Derived from Latin & Greek roots, the word "ALIFEROUS" literally translates to "To Have Wings."

Wings represent spiritual movement, rapid development, divine dominion, and strength. Just as wings allow an eagle to rise above standard atmospheric limits, Aliferous Revival Hub exists to elevate believers beyond standard societal and human limitations.

We equip men and women to soar in their God-given assignments, utilizing supernatural vision and speed to claim territories and rewrite the structures of earth.
